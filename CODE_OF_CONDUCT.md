# Code of Conduct

Quill follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good intent, and remember that people of every background and level of experience contribute here. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at the contact address listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will respond within a reasonable time and will explain any action taken.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
