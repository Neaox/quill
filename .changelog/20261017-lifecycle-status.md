Changed: a document's status now answers one question at a time. Whether a page is published is Quill's to say, and the header says it: Draft, Published, or "Published, with unpublished changes" when your draft is ahead of what readers see. Whether a page can be relied on is yours to say, with a new optional Lifecycle property: In review, Verified or Deprecated. Choosing Verified records who verified it and when ("Verified by Ada Lovelace · 2 weeks ago") and counts as a review; when the next review falls overdue, the page says "Verification lapsed" until someone reviews it again. Deprecated puts a notice under the title on every surface, "This page is deprecated.", linking to the page that replaces it if the reader may open it. You choose that page from the properties line by searching for its title. Deprecated pages rank lower in search and can't be verified until they come back through review. A review due within a fortnight now says "Review due" in the byline. Share links and the public site show the lifecycle but never who verified a page. Statuses written by earlier releases (draft, published, archived) stay in the file untouched and are no longer shown to readers.
