Added: tables you can read like a spreadsheet. A table's header row, and a totals footer, stay in view as you scroll the page, below the app bar or the site header. A wide table keeps its first column, or its row headers, in place while it scrolls sideways, and shadows show when there is more to either side. A reader can sort any column by its header (numbers, dates and text, in your language) without changing the document, and totals never sort into the data. Every table opens full screen, where it can be fitted to the width or copied as CSV or Markdown. Columns of numbers line up on the right. An optional `:::table[Caption]` wrapper names the table and sets its width (content, wide, full or a percentage), proportional column widths, row headers, a footer, what is frozen, stacking into cards on a phone, and whether sorting is on. Outside Quill it is a plain table.
