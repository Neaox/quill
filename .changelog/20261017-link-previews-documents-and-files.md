Added: links to other documents and to files (ADR-042). A link to a Quill document (`/d/<id>`, or a pasted app address) is resolved by id when a page is read, so it shows the document's live title, first paragraph and place and follows a rename or a move; a reader without access to the document sees a plain unlinked label and never its title, and an anonymous reader of a public site sees a preview only for documents published on that site. The slash item "Link to document" and pasting an app address make such a link, and the editor shows the live title in the line (the address returns when the caret is in it) and on cards. A link to a file (an image, PDF, video, audio or common document) is previewed from its response headers alone, with no body read: a card with an icon for its type, its name, type and size, and, for a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, a stored thumbnail from the existing image pipeline. The `LinkPreviewLayer` gains `internal`, and the preview's optional `file` is now filled in.
