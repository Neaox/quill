Added: Images in a workspace read from a repository now show (ADR-061). Files under the manifest's `assets` paths, and images inside its collections, are imported as attachments through the same checks as an upload (type, size, metadata removed), once per distinct content; a relative image such as `../diagrams/flow.png` draws that attachment, an SVG is drawn as a diagram, and an image that could not be imported shows its alternative text and is listed on the source's sync state with the reason.
