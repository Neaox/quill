Added: Administrators can set how Quill describes links in Settings, Links: previews off, text only or rich, whether the public site and share links show previews, how a link looks when its author chose nothing, and lists of sites to allow or never contact, each pattern checked as it is typed with the reason beside the line that is wrong; the paste offer points at the organisation's default.
Added: The link box (Ctrl+K) offers how the link is shown, the toolbar says where a link's preview came from and how old it is, warns when it will not show on the public site, and can refresh it or retry an unavailable one quietly, and the command palette has "Show link as link / inline / compact / card" for the link at the cursor.
Added: `/link compact` inserts a compact link, a link written with no display is drawn in the editor as the organisation's default, and the toolbar leaves out the card controls on a compact link; consecutive compact links sit close together like a list.
Security: A preview fetched under a looser link policy is never shown under a stricter one, including on the public site: each policy reads only its own previews, and a fetch is stored under the policy in force when it ran.
Changed: Changes to the link policy are recorded in the audit log as their own event, without the patterns.
