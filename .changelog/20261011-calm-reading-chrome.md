Changed: The frame around a document being read is calmer. The top bar is the page's own colour over a blur with a hairline below, a little taller, and set in the interface face rather than monospace capitals; Present and Share are quiet buttons, Edit is the accent's soft tint instead of a filled orange block, and the theme switch and search sit in a well with the chosen option raised. The sidebar and the contents column sit straight on the page: collection names are small headings with a document count, documents are set in a softer ink, and the current one is a soft accent selection. "On this page" comes first in the column, lists headings in full down a hairline with the current one marked on it, and revisions and signals follow in the same type. As the window narrows the bar gives up words before controls, and on a phone it holds the controls alone, so nothing scrolls sideways (`docs/design/chrome.md`).
Added: A published document that opens with its own title shows its collection's name above it as an eyebrow, styled exactly as an eyebrow an author writes, unless the author wrote one.
Added: `Button` has a `soft` variant, and `@quill/ui` has plus, pencil, present and share icons.
