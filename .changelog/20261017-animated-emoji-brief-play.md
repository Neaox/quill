Added: Animated emoji play briefly. Each one plays when it first comes into view, for at most five seconds or three loops, whichever is shorter, and then rests on its still. It plays again on hover, on focus of what holds it, or on a tap, and it pauses while off-screen or while the page is hidden. This applies in the reader, on the public site and in the editor. Readers who ask their system for reduced motion only ever see the still, with or without JavaScript (ADR-047).
Added: A personal setting, Preferences → Animated images: play briefly (the default), play on hover only, or never. It follows a member onto their organisation's public site, and reduced motion always wins over it.
Added: A poster frame for an animated GIF or PNG emoji. In the upload preview a frame scrubber, operable from the keyboard, chooses the frame it rests on and that reduced-motion readers see. It defaults to the first frame, never the last.
Changed: An animated GIF or PNG emoji is stored with a finite play count written into the file, three plays at most, so it stops even with scripts off or opened on its own. The upload is kept untouched beside it. Its frame count and loop length are recorded at upload, and older emoji read as before.
Changed: An animated WebP or AVIF emoji has no still yet and plays as uploaded until the image pipeline makes one. The emoji settings now say so.
