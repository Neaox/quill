Added: The home page now leads with what is waiting on the person (review requests, answers to theirs), then their own drafts and the documents they were just in. Recently updated is grouped by day and covers the places they work in, with a switch to everywhere; workspaces are compact rows, theirs first, with a filter and a virtualised list for large organisations. Opening a document is recorded privately for 90 days and can be cleared under Preferences.
Added: Contributors can write drafts but cannot publish or restore; editors and above can. Anyone who can edit can ask people and groups to review a draft, who approve or ask for changes; the status shows in the document header and a bell in every signed-in header lists what has happened.
Added: Administrators have a People and groups screen to create and nest groups and manage their members.
Added: Comparing two revisions draws the page as it reads with each change marked, folds long unchanged stretches, and lists the changes to go to.
Changed: The home page loads from one bounded request instead of a document list per workspace.
Fixed: A person who reached documents only through a group could lose access to all but the first document checked in a request.
Fixed: Focus rings on fields in dialogs are no longer clipped at the edges, and a picker's suggestions inside a dialog are no longer cut off.
