Fixed: Autosave no longer freezes on "saving" after one failed save. Network errors, 429, 502, 503 and 504 are retried with capped backoff (honouring Retry-After), edits made meanwhile are kept and sent, and the status says what is happening: saving, saved, offline and will retry, not saved and retrying, or couldn't save with a Retry button.
Fixed: A save whose answer was lost while the server restarted is recognised as the author's own write when it is retried, so nothing is written twice and no false conflict is reported.
Fixed: A failed background refresh no longer replaces the open editor with "Couldn't open this document". The editor stays, a quiet notice says the refresh failed, and queries retry 502, 503, 504 and 429 with backoff but never other errors.
Fixed: The browser's recovery copy of unsent edits is written in development too (React StrictMode had closed it on mount), and an edit recovered after a crash now opens in the editor rather than only being announced.
Fixed: The lock heartbeat backs off with jitter (capped at ten seconds) instead of retrying in a tight loop, pauses its retries while the browser is offline or the tab is hidden, and retries at once when either ends. Taking the lock when the editor opens is retried the same way instead of leaving it "opening" for good.
Fixed: Two tabs of the same browser can no longer silently overwrite each other's draft. The other tab learns at once that the draft moved on. "Reload the draft" really loads the newer draft and sets your unsent change aside to restore or discard, and "Keep mine" saves over it only when you choose to.
