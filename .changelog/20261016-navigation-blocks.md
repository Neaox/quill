Added: A table of contents block, `::toc`, lists the page's own headings as links: choose the heading levels (`levels=2-4`), a title, numbered or bulleted, and whether subsections fold. Its links are the headings' own permalinks, and the reader marks the section being read in it, as the "On this page" rail does (ADR-058).
Added: A child pages block, `::children`, lists the pages under this one, up to six levels deep, in the navigation's order, by title or most recently updated first, optionally with each page's summary and icon. A reader only ever sees pages they may read, and the public site lists only published pages, at its own addresses; an empty list offers an editor "Create a child page".
Added: Both blocks are in the editor's slash menu ("Table of contents", also found as "toc"; "Child pages", also found as "children"), with a live preview and a Settings panel whose every change is one step of undo.
Added: `GET /api/documents/:id/children` answers a child pages list for the reader, a `DocumentNested` event announces a page re-nested inside its collection, and `DocumentDeleted` names the parent a deleted page was listed under; the child pages cache hears both, with the others that change a list, from the in-process event bus.
