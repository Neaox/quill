Added: Search can find what somebody has published. `by:ada` finds the documents Ada has published a revision of, by any part of their name or by their exact address; `by:me` finds yours; `team:platform` finds what anyone in the Platform group (and the groups nested inside it) or unit has published. Each can be left out with a minus, and combines with words and the other filters. A person who published through Git sync is found too. Nothing is widened: you still only find what you may read. Existing documents are filled in by the upgrade, so no reindex is needed.
Security: The public site does not answer `by:` or `team:`. Otherwise `by:somebody@company.com` would tell a stranger that an address wrote a published page; the search page says it is not offered and does not run the search.
