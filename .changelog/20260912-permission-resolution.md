Security: Permission resolution now decides each principal a request holds separately and then combines them, so a deny on one principal can no longer be dropped by a list view: a document denied to a reader is hidden from the workspace tree, the flat document list, and a direct check alike.
Security: A grant to the `public` principal can no longer narrow a signed-in reader's access. Because every request holds `public`, a public viewer grant at a collection used to demote an editor granted at the workspace; it can now only widen what a reader may do.
Security: The flat workspace document list (`GET /api/workspaces/:id/documents`) now resolves each document instead of returning every document after a workspace-level check, so it no longer discloses the id, title, slug, path, and status of documents the navigation tree hides.
Fixed: A deny on the `public` principal or on a share link now closes only that channel. Taking one document off the public web — a viewer grant to `public` at a collection and a deny to `public` at that document — no longer hides it from the members who reach it through their own user or group grant. A deny on a user or a group still withdraws the document from that person however they reach it.
Fixed: Grants are validated before they are stored, so a deny above document scope and an owner grant to the `public` principal are refused rather than written and then ignored.
