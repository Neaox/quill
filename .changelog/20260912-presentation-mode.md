Added: Any published document can be presented full screen. Its top-level sections become steps at projector scale, wide and full blocks take the screen, and the only chrome is a progress bar, a section rail, and a presenter bar that fades when the room is still. The keyboard drives it (arrows, space, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End, `g` for a "go to" overlay with type-ahead, `n` for presenter notes from a `:::notes` block, Escape to leave at the section on screen), the current step travels in the URL so a refresh or a shared link lands on it, and full screen is requested on entry with a windowed fallback where it is refused.
