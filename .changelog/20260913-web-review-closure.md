Fixed: Every link in the application's chrome is a real client-side navigation again. The revision timeline and the breadcrumb rendered plain anchors, so opening an older revision, or stepping back to the workspace, reloaded the whole single-page application and threw away the cached data and the sidebar's scroll position; under the default identity the header's trail dropped its destinations altogether and was not a link at all.
Fixed: Opening a document starts at the top of it, and the back button returns to where the previous page was left; scroll restoration was never switched on.
Fixed: The editor no longer draws an empty complementary column beside the writing surface.
Fixed: One workspace is one cache entry however its address is spelled, so renaming a workspace updates the header immediately and the navigation tree is prefetched under the key the sidebar actually reads.
Fixed: The workspace home links each document by its readable address rather than by its identifier, so opening one no longer bounces through a redirect first.
Changed: `@quill/ui`'s primitives describe a destination as a route and its parameters rather than as a built URL, and take one `linkComponent` seam, so the router encodes every address and type-checks every route.
Changed: Publishing and restoring a revision now report how they ended through a promise toast with the one action that follows, as `docs/design/feedback.md` asks; a publish that could not happen because somebody else published first says so instead of settling quietly.
Changed: The "more actions" menu is one primitive on Radix's dropdown, with the keyboard model it always claimed: arrow keys, type-ahead, Escape, and focus returned to the trigger. Renaming and deleting are one dialog each rather than three near-identical copies apiece.
Changed: Inserting an image asks for its address in a labelled dialog instead of a browser prompt.
Changed: Presentation mode loads its document and body before it appears, and mounts the section on screen plus one either side rather than every section at once.
Changed: The signed-in home and the organisation page share one page frame with the workspace shell's bar height, and both can change colour scheme.
Changed: The reading route's initial bundle is 206.4 KB gzipped, down from 279.9 KB against a 250 KB budget: every workspace package declares `sideEffects`, the theme schema validator and colour doctor are unreachable from `@quill/theme/builtin`, the identity machinery moved to `@quill/ui/theme`, and the toast library is a chunk fetched after the application paints.
Fixed: On a phone, a workspace with a few hundred documents no longer gives the page thousands of pixels of empty scroll below the document: the navigation column keeps its own overflow instead of handing it to the viewport.
Changed: A row's and a collection's "more actions" control appears on hover, on focus, and while its menu is open — the treatment a collection's "+" already had — instead of drawing a permanent "⋯" beside every document in the tree.
Changed: `pnpm check:bundle` runs as the last step of `pnpm check` and blocks in CI, now that the reading route is inside its budget.
