Added: Settings → Labels and emoji, a screen of its own after Theme, holding the organisation's labels and its custom emoji. Labels are still part of the theme and save with the same revision and conflict handling; Theme is now colour, type, shape, collection colours, the preview and the doctor. The person menu, Ctrl+K ("labels", "emoji") and the home page list it.
Added: Custom emoji may be JPEG, GIF or AVIF as well as PNG, WebP or SVG, still checked against their bytes; upload your logo as the SVG your brand guide ships. The upload control lists the types it takes.
Added: Animated emoji (GIF, APNG, animated WebP and AVIF), up to 300 frames, 10 seconds and 256 KB, refused with the reason past those. A GIF or APNG gets a still of its first frame at upload, shown instead to readers who ask for less motion (ADR-047); the settings preview shows both, labelled.
Changed: The colour picker shows a colour that is none of the swatches as an extra, selected "Custom" swatch named with its value, in place of a note, for the accent and every label's colour.
Changed: The emoji preview reads "Inline with text … like this", and examples across settings, fixtures and docs use neutral names such as `:acme:` rather than one organisation's channels.
