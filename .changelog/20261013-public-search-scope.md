Fixed: A public site's search now looks only inside the site's own collection, and spends its page of results there. Before, the engine searched the whole workspace and the site discarded what was not its own afterwards, so a workspace with other public collections could leave a site's search short of results, or empty, when the site had real matches. `SearchRequest` gains a `scope` that narrows a search inside the engine's query; it can only narrow, and the visibility filter still decides what may be read.
