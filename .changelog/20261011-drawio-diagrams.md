Added: draw.io diagrams. Type `/drawio` to draw one in the draw.io editor without leaving the document, or choose "Edit diagram" on one already there; saving writes a `.drawio.svg` attachment, a new revision of the same file when you edit it, and offers "Save mine as a copy" or "Reload theirs" if somebody saved a newer one first. Diagrams are drawn inline at their own size, follow light and dark, open in the lightbox, show on the public site, and their labels are searchable. A diagram saved elsewhere with HTML labels shows as an image until it is edited here. The editor is a self-hosted draw.io (`docker compose up drawio`, `DRAWIO_URL`); diagrams.net's hosted editor is an explicit opt-in. Raw `.drawio` files can be attached.

Security: the draw.io frame is sandboxed to scripts and its own origin, messages are accepted only from its origin and window in validated shapes, and the CSP frames only the configured editor. A diagram's embedded source is inflated within fixed caps when it is read for search, and never reaches the page.
