Added: live embeds for links (ADR-042). A link shown as `embed` draws a sandboxed, lazy frame for YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma, Google Docs, Slides, Sheets and Drive, Miro, Spotify and LinkedIn posts when the organisation's link previews are `rich`; any other page stays a card. The providers are a reviewed list in code, the app's CSP `frame-src` is generated from it, and an operator can switch providers off with `LINK_EMBEDS_DISABLED`. In the editor, `Embed` joins the link display choices (toolbar, paste offer, context menu) for pages that can be embedded and says why when it cannot be, and the writing surface holds an embed's place with an inert placeholder. An export never contains a frame.
