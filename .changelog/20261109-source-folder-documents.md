Added: In a workspace read from a repository, each folder is now a page of its own, the parent of its files and folders: its `index.md`, else its `README.md`, else a page Quill writes that lists what is inside (ADR-061). Links to a folder, with or without a trailing slash, open that page. Workspaces synced before this are rearranged on their next sync and keep every page's address and history.
