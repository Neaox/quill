Added: Component blocks (ADR-060). A block is a directive with typed props and its own drawing, registered beside the Markdown extensions, and `:::endpoint{method path auth deprecated}` is the first built in. A container no block or directive claims is now drawn as a labelled frame naming it, with its content inside, where its wrapper used to vanish; a prop that does not fit is drawn as its default and the block says so; and every such problem is listed in the document's signals as a block issue, with its lines. The editor heads a block's card with its title and its directive as written, and keeps blocks byte for byte.
