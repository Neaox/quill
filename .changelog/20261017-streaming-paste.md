Changed: Pasting a long Markdown document now streams in. A "Pasting…" card appears where the paste will land the moment you paste, the first screenful follows a moment later, and the rest arrives in batches while you keep typing, scrolling and selecting. The card shows how far the paste has got, stays in sight at the bottom of the window once the paste moves past the screen, and settles on "Pasted 1,200 blocks"; screen readers hear "Pasting…" and then how much was pasted. Escape or the card's Cancel button takes the paste back, and one undo removes the whole paste however many batches it arrived in. Autosave saves the finished paste, never half of it.
Changed: Long pastes are parsed in pieces by several background workers at once, and named characters such as &copy; no longer make a paste fall back to the page.
Added: A paste that cannot be read as Markdown is taken back and says so, with Try again and Dismiss.
