Fixed: Signed-in pages no longer freeze in a workspace with a very large collection. Previously the sidebar drew every document, and when the menu code loaded, every row's menu was rebuilt at the same moment. A collection of 20,000 documents could block the page for more than a minute.
Changed: The document tree folds. A document with children has an expand and collapse button beside its link, which ArrowRight and ArrowLeft also work from. Branches start closed, except the way to the document you are reading, which opens and scrolls into view along with that document's own children. Collection names fold their whole collection. Whatever you open or close stays that way for the rest of the session.
Changed: A long branch in the tree, the move and new document pickers, and the workspace home shows 50 documents at a time, with a "Show more" button for the rest. The page around the document you are reading is shown, not the first 50.
Changed: Deep nesting in the tree stops adding indentation after five levels, so long names still fit.
Fixed: Opening an old or shortened link to a revision or a comparison now keeps that revision or comparison. Previously the corrected address dropped it and opened the latest version.
Fixed: A click that lands just as a menu, dialog or tooltip finishes loading now always reaches its button. Only the control you use loads its menu, dialog or tooltip code.
Changed: The sidebar loads its tree a slice at a time. It first loads one level, a page of each list, and the way down to the document you are reading. Further pages load as you open them, so a large workspace's sidebar is about 15 KB to load instead of 3 MB.
Added: `GET /api/workspaces/:id/tree` accepts `depth`, `limit` and `focus`, and each node and collection now says how many children or documents it has in all. Without `depth`, the whole tree is returned as before. `GET /api/workspaces/:id/tree/children` returns one page of a collection's top level or of a document's children.
Changed: The server compresses text responses (brotli or gzip) for read requests. A workspace's whole tree went from 3.2 MB to 570 KB. Responses that set a cookie or are marked no-store are never compressed.
Changed: Each level above the document you are reading shows only the way down and a few documents either side of it, with "Show earlier" and "Show more" for the rest.
Fixed: The header no longer says "Published" above a document that has never been published.
Changed: On a phone, the document tree is a sheet behind a "Browse" button instead of a band above the document. This applies when reading, editing and comparing. The sheet opens on the document you are reading, closes with its close button, Escape, or a chosen document, and keeps the page behind it out of reach while it is open.
Changed: On a phone, the button that opens the document tree is in the bar at the top of the screen, so it stays within reach however far down the page you are. To make room, rename, present, preview and share move into the document's "…" menu on phones. Edit and your account menu stay in the bar.
Changed: On a phone, the tree indents less and stops indenting after three levels, so long titles keep their room. "Show N earlier" no longer repeats its own count.
Fixed: The compare page no longer scrolls sideways on a 320-pixel screen.
Fixed: Opening the editor on a document that has no draft yet, such as one that was imported or seeded, now starts a draft instead of failing with "Draft not found". The draft starts from the published revision if there is one, or from a blank page named after the document.
Changed: On a phone there is one way into navigation: the button in the top bar. Its sheet shows the workspace's sections first, then the workspace switcher and the document tree. The separate rail band under the bar is gone on phones, which gives that space back to the document. Wider screens are unchanged.
