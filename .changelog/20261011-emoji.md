Added: Emoji shortcodes, as on GitHub: `:+1:`, `:tada:` and every other GitHub shortcode draw their emoji in documents, on the public site and in the editor. Unknown names, and anything inside code, stay as written (ADR-038).
Added: Custom emoji: an organisation can upload its own images (PNG, WebP or SVG) under names such as `:acme:` and `:acme_mark:` in Settings → Labels and emoji, previewed at text size in light and dark. A custom name replaces a GitHub one only where the two are the same.
Added: A label can lead with an emoji in place of its colour dot: the emoji with the label's own name, or one chosen for it under Labels. A table of channels can show each channel's logo.
Changed: Rendered bodies are at render version 5, so every document re-renders once to pick up emoji.
Fixed: Saving a document no longer escapes colons that could never start a directive: `:smile:`, `a:b:c` and `10:30:00` are written back exactly as typed, instead of as `\:smile:` and `a\:b:c`.
Fixed: Saving a document keeps a bare address bare: `https://example.com`, `www.example.com` and `user@example.com` are no longer rewritten as `<https://example.com>`, and an address written in angle brackets keeps them.
