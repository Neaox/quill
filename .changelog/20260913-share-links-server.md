Added: A document can be shared with somebody who has no account. A link covers that document, or that document and everything nested under it, carries a view role and an optional expiry, and can be revoked — closing on the very next request. The token is 256 random bits, only its SHA-256 is stored, it is shown to its creator exactly once, and it never appears in a log line or an audit row (`docs/architecture/api-contract-share-links.md`). Creating, listing and revoking need `manage` on the document; reading through a link needs no account, shows the published body and nothing else about the organisation, is `noindex`, and is rate limited per address. Unknown, expired, revoked, out of scope, and unpublished are all answered identically, so a token cannot be probed for. The server half only: the share dialog and the share-link page come with the web work.
Added: `SHARE_LINKS=on|off` (default on) decides whether an organisation has share links at all. Turning it off refuses new links and closes existing ones on their next use, because the policy is asked on every use rather than cached. It moves to the organisation's settings when the settings store lands.
