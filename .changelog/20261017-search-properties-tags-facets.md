Added: Tags can be nested with a slash. A page tagged `area/billing` is found by `tag:area/billing`, and also by `tag:area`, which finds everything tagged `area` or anything beneath it. The tag suggestions offer every level.
Added: Search can look at any other front-matter field on purpose with `prop:key=value`, for example `prop:severity=high` or `-prop:team=sre`. These fields are never searched by plain words and never suggested; pages gain them in the index when they are next published or after `quill reindex`, so `prop:` finds nothing on a page until then.
Added: A search can report what its results contain. `GET /api/search?facets=tag,status,collection` adds the most common tags (with their parents), statuses and collections among everything the search matched, with counts, so a person can narrow the results. Pages you may not read are never counted.
Security: The public site does not answer `prop:`, as it does not answer `by:` and `team:`; the search page says searching by person, team or property is not offered.
