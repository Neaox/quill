Added: A workspace read from a repository now takes its structure from the repository's `.quill/workspace.yaml` (ADR-061): its collections by slug and title, paths that may be globs, `exclude`, `redirects` that keep a rewritten file's address, and each collection's order (`title`, `filename`, `newest` or `oldest`) after any front matter `order`. Only what the manifest names is fetched, and a manifest that does not read is reported with its line while the workspace stays as it was.
Changed: A sourced workspace's collections and paths may now change after it has synced; the next sync places every document again, archiving what the new layout leaves out and retiring collections the manifest no longer names.
Changed: Documents in the navigation tree are now ordered by their sort key and then oldest first (ADR-049), rather than in no particular order.
