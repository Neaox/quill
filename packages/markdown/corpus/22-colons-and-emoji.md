# Colons and emoji

Shortcodes: :smile: and :+1: and :acme: stay as they were written.

:smile: opens a line, and a line can end with one :+1:

Times like 10:30:00, ratios like a:b:c, and the status:ok of a check.

A badge beside an emoji, :smile: next to :ok[Built], keeps both.

An address such as <https://x.y/a:b> is not a directive, and nor is https://x.y/a:b written bare.

Bare addresses stay bare: https://example.com, www.example.com/docs and user@example.com.
