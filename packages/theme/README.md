# @quill/theme

Themes are declarative documents, not CSS (ADR-028). This package owns the document schema, the built-in identities, the OKLCH palette generator, and the theme doctor.

- `validateThemeDocument(input)` — TypeBox schema checked by ajv, returning structured issues.
- `generateTheme(document)` — `{ light, dark, report }`, where the token maps are exactly the custom properties `packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css` resolves, plus `--token-*`, type, the reading setting (`--text-reading`, `--leading-reading`, `--layout-content` and the `--prose-*` scale and link properties, from `type.scale` and `type.links`), shape, and layout.
- `toCss(generated, { selector })` — the `:root`, `prefers-color-scheme`, and `data-theme` blocks, in that resolution order.
- `fontFaceCss(document, { curatedHref })` — the `@font-face` rules for the faces a theme names, for a page with no bundle behind it; `CURATED_FACE_FILES` is the catalogue of each curated face's Fontsource files, held to `packages/ui/src/styles/fonts.css` by `fonts-catalogue.test.ts`.
- `runThemeDoctor(document)` — every rule in `docs/design/colour-rules.md` as pass, adjusted, or warn. AA text contrast is enforced by default and can be made advisory; everything else advises.
- Each doctor result carries its `evidence`: the pairs it measured (both colours, ratio and APCA Lc against the target, per scheme), the colours it held to a band or budget, and the colours the generator moved, as set and as shipped. `bySeverity` and `severityCounts` order results and counts warnings first.
- `suggestFixes(document, ruleId)` — concrete changes to the seeds or overrides that clear a warning, each re-generated and re-graded before it is offered (the rule no longer warns, nothing that held starts to, and no remaining warning moves further from its target, measured by `shortfall` over its evidence); `applyThemeChange` makes one.
- Suggested fixes keep the brand's feel (`feel.ts`): a fix never turns the accent's or a status colour's hue, never takes a recognisably tinted brand colour below `RECOGNISABLE_CHROMA` (in OKLab, the distance from the grey at the same lightness), and the closest to what the tenant had comes first, by an OKLab ΔE that counts chroma and hue twice as heavily as lightness. A warning that cannot be cleared that way can be accepted: `acknowledged` in the theme document lists rule ids, and the doctor reports those as accepted (`severityOf`).
- Washes (the accent and status colours mixed into the page) have their own chroma budget, `WASH_CHROMA_BUDGET`: C 0.03 in light and C 0.045 in dark, calibrated on the dark accent tints of Linear, Vercel and Fumadocs (roughly C 0.03 to 0.05 at low lightness). The panel budget of C 0.02 left a brand-tinted dark wash nowhere to go but grey. Dark re-derivation leaves washes to that budget rather than to the light wash's chroma.
- `OFFERED_THEMES` / `BUILTIN_THEMES` / `DEFAULT_THEME` — the five offered identities: Instrument (the default), Calm, Editorial and Grid (the base themes of `docs/research/2026-10-11-documentation-design-survey.md`, Grid being the lockstay portal's look), and Atelier; `BUILTIN_THEMES` also resolves a superseded id (Press → Editorial, `successorOf`), because a saved fork may name it.
- `extractSeedsFromColours`, `suggestBase`, `forkTheme` — the onboarding flow's three steps.

Palettes are generated, never hand-picked: a neutral ramp at the lightness bands of section 3, accent variants nudged until the contrast floors hold, a status set at matched lightness and chroma, and syntax colours at fixed hues. Dark mode is a re-derivation with the chroma reduced, never an inversion. Every place the generator changed what the seeds asked for is reported.

Nothing here touches the DOM. Tenants get the colours they asked for wherever those colours are safe, and a derived colour with a named reason wherever they are not.
