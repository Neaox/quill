# Escalation matrix

Find the level in [Severity definitions](doc:Severity definitions), then use this table. If you are unsure between two levels, pick the higher one and say why in a private note on the ticket.

::::wide
:::table[Who to escalate to]{columns="1fr 2fr 2fr 1fr"}
| Severity | Escalate to | How | Update customer every |
| :------- | :---------- | :-- | --------------------: |
| :bad[Sev 1] | On-call engineer, then Marcus Lee | Page through PagerDuty and post in #incidents | 30 min |
| :warn[Sev 2] | Platform team lead | Post in #support-escalations with the ticket link | 1 hour |
| :accent[Sev 3] | Support lead on shift | Tag the ticket `escalate` | 1 working day |
| :ok[Sev 4] | Nobody; work it in the queue | Normal ticket | 3 working days |
| :security[Security] | Aisha Khan, at any severity | Page the security on-call; do not discuss in public channels | 30 min |
:::
::::

Tell the customer who owns the problem and when they will hear next, before you hand it over.

At weekends, Sev 2 goes to the engineer on call rather than the team lead, and the support lead on call replaces the lead on shift.
