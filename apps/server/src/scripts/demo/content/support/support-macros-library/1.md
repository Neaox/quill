# Support macros library

These are the macros agents use most, with the wording customers read. Treat them as a first draft: change the details to match the ticket, keep it warm, and never send one without reading the customer's message again. Anything in square brackets is yours to fill in.

Macros live in Zendesk under **Support macros**. If you improve one, edit it here first and tell Tom Becker, so the library and Zendesk stay in step.

## Before you send one

1. Use the customer's name and their words. If they wrote "the board is empty", say "the board is empty", not "the dashboard shows no data".
2. Delete every sentence that does not apply. A macro with a paragraph about the wrong integration is worse than no macro.
3. Add one concrete detail from their workspace: a row number, a timestamp, a connection name. That is what shows a person wrote it.
4. Set the ticket status deliberately. Pending when you wait for the customer, On hold when you wait for engineering, Solved only when they have what they asked for.

::::accordion{name=macros}
:::details[First reply to a new ticket]
Use for any ticket where you need a few minutes to investigate.

> Hi [First name],
>
> Thanks for getting in touch. I've read your message and I'm looking into [the problem in their words]. I'll write back by [time and timezone] with what I find, even if it's only an update.
>
> [Your name]
:::

:::details[Feedback is not appearing]
Use when imported or synced feedback is missing. Point to [Why isn't my feedback appearing?](doc:Why isn't my feedback appearing?) only after you have checked the basics yourself.

> Hi [First name],
>
> I've checked your workspace and the [source] connection last synced at [time]. [Describe what you found: for example, 214 rows were skipped because the Created column was empty.]
>
> I've [what you did]. New items should appear within 15 minutes. If they haven't by then, reply here and I'll take another look.
:::

:::details[Zendesk sync has stalled]
Use when the Zendesk connection shows "Syncing" for more than 2 hours.

> Hi [First name],
>
> Your Zendesk connection has been stuck since [time]. This is usually an expired API token on the Zendesk side. Could you reconnect it under **Settings > Integrations > Zendesk**? Nothing is lost: we pick up from the last ticket we received.
>
> If it still stalls after reconnecting, tell me and I'll ask our engineers to look at your sync directly.
:::

:::details[CSV import failed]
Use when an import ends with errors.

> Hi [First name],
>
> Your import of [file name] stopped at row [number]. The reason was [reason]. I've attached a list of the rows that failed with the exact error for each. Fix those rows and import the file again; the rows that succeeded won't be duplicated.
>
> Files can be up to 25 MB or 100,000 rows. If yours is bigger, split it and I'm happy to check each part.
:::

:::details[Refund within the window]
Use for refunds you can grant yourself. Check the [Refund and credit policy](doc:Refund and credit policy) first.

> Hi [First name],
>
> I've refunded the [amount] charged on [date] to your [card]. It will appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days. Your workspace stays active until [end date].
>
> I'm sorry it wasn't the right fit. If something specific was missing, I'd like to hear it.
:::

:::details[Credit for an outage]
Use for downtime credits on Business and Enterprise plans.

> Hi [First name],
>
> You're right that [date] fell below our uptime commitment: Tidepool was available [percentage] of that month. I've applied a credit of [percentage]% of your monthly fee, [amount], to your next invoice.
>
> I'm sorry for the disruption it caused your team.
:::

:::details[Data export ready]
Use after the export link has been sent. See [Data export and deletion requests](doc:Data export and deletion requests).

> Hi [First name],
>
> Your export is on its way to [owner's email]. The link works for 7 days and the file contains your feedback, themes and roadmap items as CSV and JSON.
>
> If the link expires before you get to it, reply and I'll generate a new one.
:::

:::details[Invoice or VAT request]
Use when a customer needs a corrected invoice. Finance issues the document; you start it.

> Hi [First name],
>
> I can get that corrected. Please reply with the company name, billing address and VAT or tax number exactly as they should appear. Our finance team will issue a new invoice within 2 working days and I'll send it here. The old invoice will be voided, so there will be no double charge.
:::

:::details[Closing a resolved ticket]
Use when the customer has confirmed the fix, or has not replied for 5 days after you did.

> Hi [First name],
>
> I'm glad [the problem] is sorted. I'll mark this as solved, but replying to this email will reopen it, and I'll see it. If anything else comes up, we're here.
>
> [Your name]
:::

:::details[Holding reply for an escalation]
Use when you have handed the ticket to engineering.

> Hi [First name],
>
> I've passed your case to our [team], who can look at this in more depth than I can. [Name] is working on it and I'll update you by [time and timezone], whether or not it's fixed by then.
:::
