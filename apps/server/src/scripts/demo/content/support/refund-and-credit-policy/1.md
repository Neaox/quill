# Refund and credit policy

Customers should never have to argue to get back money we clearly owe them. This policy sets what you can grant yourself, what needs a lead, and what needs finance. If a case is not covered, ask Tom Becker before you promise anything.

## Refunds

- **Monthly plans** (Team at $29 per seat, Business at $59 per seat): a full refund of the first charge if the customer asks within 14 days of starting. After that we do not refund the current month, and cancelling takes effect at the end of the period.
- **Annual plans:** a full refund within 30 days of purchase or renewal. After 30 days there is no refund, but you can offer to move the customer to monthly billing at the next renewal.
- **Our mistakes:** a duplicate charge or a charge after a confirmed cancellation is always refunded in full, whatever its age, up to 12 months back.
- **Enterprise:** the contract decides. Check the order form, then ask the account manager.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and arrive in 5 to 10 business days. Tell the customer that, so they do not write back on day three.

## Credits for downtime

Business and Enterprise customers have a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment for the Tidepool web app and API. When we miss it, the customer is owed a credit against next month's invoice:

| Uptime in the month | Credit |
| :------------------ | -----: |
| Below 99.9% | 10% of the monthly fee |
| Below 99.5% | 25% |
| Below 99.0% | 50% |

The customer must ask within 30 days of the end of the month. Check the status page history and confirm the figure with the platform team before you quote it. Free and Team plans have no uptime commitment, but a goodwill credit of up to one month is reasonable after a long outage; see [Severity definitions](doc:Severity definitions) for what counts as one.

## Who can approve what

:::table[Approval limits]{width=70%}
| Amount (refund or credit) | Who can approve | Decision within |
| :------------------------ | :-------------- | --------------: |
| Up to $100 | Any agent | Immediately |
| $101 to $1,000 | Support lead on shift | Same working day |
| $1,001 to $5,000 | Tom Becker | 1 working day |
| Over $5,000 | Finance, with Tom | 3 working days |
:::

Add the amount and the reason to the ticket as a private note every time, even for small ones. Finance reconciles from those notes at month end.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not split one request into several smaller ones to stay under your limit. Approvals are per customer per month, not per ticket.

## Wording

Say what you are doing, the amount, and when it will arrive. "I've refunded the $87 charge from 3 October to your Visa ending 4242. It will show on your statement within 5 to 10 business days." If you cannot grant the request, say what you can do instead, as in [Handling an angry customer](doc:Handling an angry customer).
