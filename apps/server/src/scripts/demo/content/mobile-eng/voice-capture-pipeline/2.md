# Voice capture pipeline

A voice note in Skiff is a person talking for between 10 seconds and 5 minutes, often in a noisy place, often with no signal. By the time they put the phone down the audio has to be safe on disk, and sometime later it has to come back as text attached to the note. This page follows the audio through each stage and records the budget for each. The product behaviour is in [Spec: Skiff voice notes](doc:Spec: Skiff voice notes).

## Recording

The native recorder (see [Skiff app architecture](doc:Skiff app architecture)) captures mono audio at 16 kHz and encodes it as AAC-LC at 64 kbps. 16 kHz is enough for speech and keeps a minute of audio at about 480 KB. Audio is cut into 10-second chunk files as it is recorded. We chose 10 seconds because a killed app loses at most one chunk, and because smaller files made the upload loop chatty: at 2 seconds a 3-minute note was 90 requests.

The recorder holds the audio session for the whole recording, so a phone call or a Bluetooth headset connecting pauses the note instead of ending it. Resuming appends new chunks to the same note.

## Trimming silence

Since 2026.8 a voice activity detector runs over each finished chunk, on a background thread, before it is queued for upload. Stretches of silence longer than 1.5 seconds are shortened to 400 ms. Nothing is trimmed from the original on disk until the upload is confirmed, so a bad trim can be redone. On the September data this cut uploaded audio by 38% and the transcription cost by about the same, and the word error rate on our test set was unchanged at 9.1%. People who dictate slowly, like the dispatchers at Lantern Logistics, benefit most.

## Upload and transcription

Chunks are uploaded one at a time, in order, by the sync engine described in [Offline sync design](doc:Offline sync design). When the last chunk is confirmed, Tidepool concatenates them, runs transcription and writes the transcript onto the note. The device receives it on its next pull, within about a minute in the foreground.

:::table[Stage budgets]{width=wide caption=bottom}
| Stage | Runs on | Budget | Typical |
| :---- | :------ | -----: | ------: |
| Start recording after tap | Phone | 300 ms | 180 ms |
| Chunk written to disk | Phone | 50 ms | 12 ms |
| Silence trimming of one chunk | Phone | 100 ms | 35 ms |
| Upload of a 3-minute note | Network | 20 s | 7 s on LTE |
| Transcription of 3 minutes | Tidepool | 30 s | 14 s |
| Transcript visible after the upload completes | Phone | 90 s | 40 s |
:::

## Failure behaviour

- **Disk full.** The recorder stops, keeps what it has and tells the user how long the note is. A partial note is better than none.
- **Microphone permission revoked mid-note.** Same: keep the chunks, mark the note as "Recording stopped".
- **Transcription fails.** The note keeps its audio and shows "Transcript unavailable". Tidepool retries three times over 15 minutes before giving up.
- **Language not supported.** The note is kept as audio only, and the transcript line explains why.

> [!NOTE]
> We never delete audio from the phone until the server confirms the final chunk and the transcript has been applied. Both are visible in the `attachment` table's `state` column.
