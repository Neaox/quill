# Engineering hiring plan 2027

Engineering is 97 people today, out of 240 at Acme. This plan proposes 14 net new engineering hires in 2027, taking us to 111 by December. It was built with Marcus Lee and the other engineering managers and is reviewed with finance each quarter.

## Where the hires go

:::table[Net new engineering hires by team and quarter]{width=wide footer=1 caption=bottom}
| Team | Today | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Adds | End 2027 |
| :--- | ----: | -: | -: | -: | -: | ---: | -------: |
| Platform | 22 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 25 |
| Tidepool web | 41 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 46 |
| Skiff mobile | 18 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 3 | 21 |
| Data and ML | 12 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 14 |
| Security | 4 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 5 |
| Total | 97 | 6 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 14 | 111 |
:::

These are net adds. We expect roughly 9 engineers to leave during the year, a rate of about 9%, and each of those is backfilled on top of the table. That makes about 23 offers to accept in total.

## Why these teams

- **Tidepool web** carries the roadmap for public roadmaps and feedback clustering, and has the longest backlog.
- **Platform** needs a second engineer on each of the multi-region and database work streams before the eu-west-1 failover is tested end to end.
- **Skiff mobile** is three people short of covering voice notes and offline sync with two engineers each.
- **Data and ML** adds capacity for the clustering rework.
- **Security** adds one engineer to own the SOC 2 Type II evidence pipeline.

## Levels

Most roles are E4 and E5. We hire at E6 only where a team has no one who can lead a cross-team design, and at E3 only through the graduate programme, up to two a year. Bands are in [Engineering levels and bands](doc:Engineering levels and bands).

## Capacity

Two recruiters can run about 30 loops a month between them. At the current 28% onsite-to-offer ratio and 80% acceptance, 23 offers needs roughly 100 onsite loops over the year, which fits. Interviewers should follow the [Interview rubric for engineers](doc:Interview rubric for engineers).
