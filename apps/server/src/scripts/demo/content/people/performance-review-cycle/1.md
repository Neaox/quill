# Performance review cycle

Acme runs one formal review a year, in the last quarter, and an informal check-in in June. This page covers the 2026 annual cycle for all 240 employees. Engineers are assessed against the expectations for their level in [Engineering levels and bands](doc:Engineering levels and bands); other functions use the equivalent ladders held by their own leads.

## Timeline

| Step | Who | Date |
| :--- | :-- | :--- |

## What you write

**Self-review.** Four short answers: what you delivered, what you are proudest of, where you fell short, and what you want to do next year. Keep it to one page. Specific beats comprehensive; a pull request link is worth three adjectives.

**Peer feedback.** You will be asked to give feedback to up to three colleagues and may be asked by up to three. Say what they did, what effect it had, and what they could do differently. Feedback is shared with the manager and summarised to the person without names unless you agree otherwise.

**Manager assessment.** Managers write an assessment that cites evidence from the year, not from the last six weeks, and recommends a rating.

## Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
| :----- | :------ |
| Exceeds | Consistently delivered at the next level up |
| Strong | Exceeded the expectations of the current level in most areas |
| Meets | Delivered what is expected of the level |
| Developing | Met some expectations; a clear plan to close the gaps is agreed |
| Below | Did not meet expectations; a performance plan follows |

There is no forced distribution. In 2025 about 12% of employees were rated Exceeds, 31% Strong, 51% Meets and 6% Developing or Below, and we treat that as a sanity check rather than a target.

## Calibration

Department heads and managers review every Exceeds and every Developing or Below rating together, and a sample of the rest. Ratings are changed only with a written reason. A manager does not discuss another team's ratings outside the session.

> [!WARNING]
> Never tell someone their rating before calibration has finished. A rating that changes after it has been shared does more damage than waiting two weeks.

## Pay and promotion

Pay changes follow ratings and the person's position in their band. Promotion cases are submitted with the manager assessment and decided in calibration. Questions go to Dana Okafor.
