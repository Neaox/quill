# Engineering levels and bands

Acme's engineering ladder has five levels, E3 to E7. A level describes the scope of work someone does reliably, not their years of service. Each level has one salary band, in US dollars, effective 1 July 2026, adjusted by location tier from 1 October 2026.

## The levels

| Level | Title | Scope | Typical experience |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | -----------------: |
| E3 | Engineer | Completes well-defined tasks with guidance | 0 to 2 years |
| E4 | Software engineer | Owns features end to end within one team | 2 to 5 years |
| E5 | Senior engineer | Owns a system, mentors, designs across two teams | 5 to 9 years |
| E6 | Staff engineer | Sets technical direction for a product area | 8 or more years |
| E7 | Principal engineer | Sets direction for the company's engineering | 12 or more years |

Experience is a rough guide, never a requirement. Jonas Weber is a staff engineer with seven years behind him.

## Salary bands

The figures below are Tier 1 bands. We moved every band up by about 4% on 1 July after benchmarking against a survey of 31 comparable B2B software companies. Existing employees below the new minimum were brought up to it in the July payroll.

| Level | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum | Annual bonus target |
| :---- | ------: | -------: | ------: | ------------------: |
| E3 | $116,000 | $131,000 | $146,000 | 5% |
| E4 | $140,000 | $158,000 | $176,000 | 5% |
| E5 | $170,000 | $192,000 | $214,000 | 8% |
| E6 | $205,000 | $231,500 | $258,000 | 10% |
| E7 | $245,000 | $276,500 | $308,000 | 15% |

New hires are offered between the minimum and the midpoint unless the interview loop scored clearly above the bar. Nobody is hired above the midpoint without approval from Dana Okafor. Offers above the maximum are not possible; if someone is worth more, the right answer is a higher level.

## Location tiers

Salary is multiplied by the tier of the employee's home address. Employees outside the United States are paid in local currency on separate bands that follow the same structure.

| Tier | Where | Multiplier |
| :--- | :---- | ---------: |
| 1 | San Francisco Bay Area, New York City | 100% |
| 2 | Other large US metros, including Seattle, Boston, Austin and Denver | 92% |
| 3 | Everywhere else in the United States | 85% |

An E5 in Austin therefore has a band of $156,400 to $196,880. No one's pay was reduced when tiers were introduced.

## Equity

New hires receive a restricted stock grant vesting over four years with a one-year cliff. The value at grant, in US dollars, is the same in every tier.

| Level | Grant at hire |
| :---- | ------------: |
| E3 | $40,000 |
| E4 | $80,000 |
| E5 | $160,000 |
| E6 | $300,000 |
| E7 | $520,000 |

## Promotion

A promotion needs sustained evidence at the next level for two consecutive review cycles, written up by the manager and calibrated across engineering. A promotion moves the salary to at least the new level's minimum, in the employee's tier. The evidence expected is described in the [Performance review cycle](doc:Performance review cycle), and the hiring bar for each level in the [Interview rubric for engineers](doc:Interview rubric for engineers).
