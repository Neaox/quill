# Penetration test summary 2026

Fernleaf Security carried out our annual external penetration test from 7 to 25 September 2026. This is the summary for staff and for the SOC 2 auditor; the full 48-page report is held by the security lead and shared with customers under NDA. Status is as of 12 October 2026.

## Scope and method

The testers worked grey-box, with two Tidepool accounts in separate workspaces and a Skiff test account, over 15 working days with two consultants. In scope: the Tidepool web app, the public API, the embeddable feedback widget, the Skiff iOS and Android apps, and the staging environment. Out of scope: social engineering, denial of service and our third-party suppliers.

## Findings

Targets come from the vulnerability deadlines in the [Information security policy](doc:Information security policy): 7 days for Critical, 30 for High, 90 for Medium. The clock started at the date the testers reported each finding.

## What we are changing

- Every object lookup in the public API now goes through one authorisation helper, with a test that tries each endpoint across workspaces.
- Webhook deliveries pass through an egress proxy that blocks private and link-local ranges.
- Skiff keeps tokens in the iOS Keychain and Android Keystore. The fix ships in release 2026.10 (see [Release notes 2026.10](doc:Release notes 2026.10)).

:::callout[F-01 in context]{type=important}
We found no evidence in logs that anyone used the F-01 flaw before the test. The testers reported it the day they found it, on 15 September, and the fix was in production on 21 September, six days later.
:::

The evidence from this test, and from our tracking of the open items, feeds the CC7.1 row in [SOC 2 Type II readiness](doc:SOC 2 Type II readiness).
