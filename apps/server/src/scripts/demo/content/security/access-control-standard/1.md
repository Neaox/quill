# Access control standard

This standard turns the access rules in the [Information security policy](doc:Information security policy) into things you can do and check. It covers every Acme employee and contractor and every system that holds Confidential or Restricted data. It maps to SOC 2 controls CC6.1, CC6.2 and CC6.3.

## Principles

Access follows the role, not the person. Someone gets what their role group grants, and anything beyond that needs a request with a business reason, approved by their manager and by the system owner.

## System tiers

| Tier | Systems | Approval | Authentication | Review |
| :--- | :------ | :------- | :------------- | :----- |
| 1 | AWS production accounts, production databases, KMS, the secrets manager | Manager and security lead | Hardware key | Quarterly |
| 2 | GitHub, CI/CD, Datadog, Zendesk, Google Workspace admin | Manager and system owner | Hardware key for admins, otherwise passkey or authenticator | Quarterly |
| 3 | Slack, Linear, Google Workspace, internal wikis | Role group, automatic | Passkey or authenticator | Annual |

## Joiners, movers and leavers

- **Joiners.** The manager files one request on the person's first day; role access is granted within one working day.
- **Movers.** When someone changes team, their old role group is removed on the day the new one is granted. Exceptions need a date.
- **Leavers.** People operations notifies the security team before the person's last day. Tier 1 and Tier 2 access is removed by the end of their last working day, and everything else within 24 hours.

## Production access

Engineers have no standing access to production data, with one exception: on-call engineers have read access to the replica databases for the length of their shift. Anything beyond that, such as a write session, is approved by the security lead for up to 24 hours, needs a linked ticket, and is recorded. Every session is reviewed by the security team the next working day.

Shared accounts are not allowed. Service accounts are owned by a named team, scoped to a single purpose, and have their credentials rotated every 90 days.

## Quarterly review

System owners confirm every account on their Tier 1 and Tier 2 systems each quarter, and the security team checks that nothing was missed. Accounts that nobody can justify are removed within 5 working days. Results are published in the quarterly review, such as [Quarterly access review: Q3 2026](doc:Quarterly access review: Q3 2026).

## Exceptions

Break-glass accounts for production exist in a sealed vault. Using one pages the security lead and requires a written explanation within 24 hours.
