## What we found

Of the 22 changes, 13 were people who had moved teams and kept access to their old one, 6 were contractors whose engagements had ended, and 3 were service accounts with no owner. Every removal was completed by 9 October, inside the 5 working day limit.

Google Workspace has more accounts than Acme has employees (240) because it includes 12 contractors and shared mailboxes with named owners.
