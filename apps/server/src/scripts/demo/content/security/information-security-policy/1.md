# Information security policy

::toc{min=2 max=2}

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how Acme Software protects the information it holds for its customers and for itself. It applies to every employee, contractor and intern, to every system that stores or processes Acme or customer data (Tidepool, Skiff and the internal tools that support them), and to every third party that handles that data on our behalf.

The policy is owned by Aisha Khan, security lead, and approved by the executive team once a year. Detailed requirements live in standards such as the [Access control standard](doc:Access control standard); where a standard is stricter than this policy, the standard wins.

## Principles

1. **Least privilege.** People and services get the access their role needs and nothing more.
2. **Defence in depth.** No single control, human or technical, is the only thing between an attacker and customer data.
3. **Secure by default.** The safe configuration is the one you get without asking.
4. **Evidence over assertion.** If we cannot show a control worked, for an auditor or for a customer, we treat it as not having worked.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :------------- |
| Security lead | Owns this policy, the risk register and the security programme |
| Engineering managers | Make sure their teams follow secure development practice and complete reviews on time |
| People operations | Background checks, onboarding and offboarding, security training records |
| Every employee | Follow this policy, complete training, report anything suspicious within one hour |

## Data classification

All information is classified, and the classification decides how it may be stored and shared.

| Class | Examples | Handling |
| :---- | :------- | :------- |
| Public | Marketing site, public roadmaps, developer documentation | No restrictions |
| Internal | Handbook, meeting notes, internal roadmaps | Acme accounts only |
| Confidential | Source code, financial results, contracts, personnel records | Named groups; encrypted at rest; no personal devices |
| Restricted | Customer feedback data, voice-note audio and transcripts, credentials and keys | Production access only through approved tooling; every access logged |

Customer feedback is Restricted by default. Customers such as Harbourview Health and Pinecrest Bank have contractual terms that add to this, and the account team records them in the customer's file.

## Access control

Access is granted by role, approved by a manager, reviewed every quarter and removed on departure. Multi-factor authentication is required on every system that supports it. Administrators and anyone with production access must use a hardware security key. Details, including timings, are in the [Access control standard](doc:Access control standard).

## Data protection

- Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or later, and TLS 1.3 wherever the client supports it.
- Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Database volumes, object storage and backups use keys held in AWS KMS, with automatic annual rotation.
- Production data is not copied to laptops or to staging. Staging uses generated data.
- Passwords are never stored by Acme in recoverable form; Tidepool hashes them with Argon2id.
- Secrets live in the secrets manager and are rotated at least every 90 days (see [Secrets rotation](doc:Secrets rotation)).

## Secure development

- Every change is reviewed by a second engineer before merge, and protected branches cannot be pushed to directly.
- Dependency scanning and static analysis run on every pull request; a Critical or High finding blocks the merge.
- Production deploys come from the pipeline only. Engineers do not deploy from laptops.
- Threat modelling is required for any feature that adds a new way for customer data to enter or leave Tidepool, such as a new integration.
- An independent penetration test is carried out every year. The latest is summarised in [Penetration test summary 2026](doc:Penetration test summary 2026).

## Vulnerability management

Vulnerabilities are scored with CVSS and adjusted for our own exposure. The clock starts when the issue is confirmed.

| Severity | Fix within |
| :------- | ---------: |
| Critical | 14 days |
| High | 30 days |
| Medium | 90 days |
| Low | Next planned release, or accepted with a reason |

Anything that cannot meet its deadline needs a documented exception (see below), approved by the security lead.

## Logging and monitoring

Authentication events, administrative actions and access to Restricted data are logged centrally and kept for 13 months, the first 90 days immediately searchable. Alerts for suspicious sign-ins and privilege changes go to the security on-call, who acknowledge within 15 minutes during business hours.

## Incident response

A suspected security incident is reported to the security channel or to security@acme.example immediately. Incidents are handled under the [Incident response process](doc:Incident response process). If customer data may have been exposed, the security lead and the executive team decide on customer notification within 24 hours of confirming it, and in any case within the period our contracts require, which is 72 hours for most customers.

## Availability and recovery

Tidepool runs in AWS `us-east-1` with a failover region in `eu-west-1`. Our recovery objectives are a recovery point of 15 minutes and a recovery time of four hours. Failover is exercised at least twice a year and documented in [Multi-region failover](doc:Multi-region failover). Backups are tested by restoring them, quarterly.

## Suppliers

No new supplier receives Confidential or Restricted data until it has been assessed under [Vendor risk assessment](doc:Vendor risk assessment). Tier 1 suppliers are reassessed every year.

## People

- Background checks are completed before the start date, where the law allows.
- Security awareness training is completed within 5 working days of joining and once a year after.
- Company laptops are encrypted, managed and set to lock after five minutes. Personal devices may not hold Confidential or Restricted data.
- When someone leaves, all access is removed on their last day.

## Exceptions

An exception to this policy or a standard needs a written request that names the risk, the compensating controls and an end date no more than 6 months away. The security lead approves or rejects it, and all open exceptions are reviewed each quarter.

## Enforcement

Breaches of this policy are handled under the disciplinary procedure in the handbook. Deliberate or repeated breaches can lead to dismissal. Reporting a mistake promptly is never grounds for discipline on its own.

## Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and after any significant incident or change to our systems. Approved by the executive team on 5 January 2026.
