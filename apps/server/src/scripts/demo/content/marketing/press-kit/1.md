## About Acme Software

> Acme Software makes Tidepool, the place where product teams collect customer feedback, cluster it into themes and publish a public roadmap, and Skiff, the iOS and Android app for capturing feedback in the field. Founded in 2016, Acme has 240 employees and more than 1,800 customer teams, from startups to hospitals and retailers.
