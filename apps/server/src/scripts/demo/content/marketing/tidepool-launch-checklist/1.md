# Tidepool launch checklist

Use this for every Tidepool release that customers will hear about, which in practice means the monthly release and any feature with its own announcement. For a Skiff release use the [Skiff 2.0 launch plan](doc:Skiff 2.0 launch plan) as the model instead. Copy the list into the launch channel and tick it off there.

## One week before

- [ ] Positioning one-liner agreed with product
- [ ] Release notes draft reviewed against the [release notes](doc:Release notes 2026.10) format
- [ ] Help articles drafted and handed to support
- [ ] Launch date and embargo time (09:00 ET) put on the calendar
- [ ] Customer quote approved in writing, with the exact wording

## Launch day

- [ ] Feature flag is on for all workspaces and confirmed by engineering
- [ ] Blog post, release notes and in-app announcement publish together
- [ ] Email sent to opted-in contacts; unsubscribe link tested
- [ ] Social posts scheduled for 09:30 ET
- [ ] Support has the macro and a named contact in the launch channel

## Within a week

- [ ] Adoption and trial numbers shared with product
- [ ] Questions from support turned into help article fixes
- [ ] Anything we got wrong corrected in public, with a note
