# Interview notes: Harbourview Health

**Date:** 9 September 2026, 45 minutes, remote
**Interviewed:** Head of patient research (Business plan, 300 seats), and one research coordinator
**Interviewers:** Priya Nair, Sofia Alvarez's researcher

## Context

Harbourview runs six hospitals and collects feedback from patient surveys, complaints and clinician interviews. They moved from spreadsheets to Tidepool in March and now hold about 90,000 items.

## What we heard

- Themes are only useful if they can be defended. A clinical director asked "why are these two comments together?" and nobody could answer.
- Patient identifiers sometimes appear in free text. They redact by hand before import and want that to be automatic.
- Procurement needs data to stay in the EU. They are happy with failover to `eu-west-1` but not with `us-east-1` as the home region.
- They love the public roadmap for staff, but will not publish one to patients.

> "If I tell a nurse we're fixing something, I need to show her where it is."
