# Competitive landscape

Last updated for the leadership offsite. Sources: sales call notes from 41 competitive deals since January, public pricing pages, trial accounts we opened in June, and eleven customer interviews. Treat the ratings as our judgement, not a benchmark.

## Who we meet

Four vendors appear in almost every deal. None is a perfect substitute, and the one that shows up most (Loopwise) is the one we lose to least.

Percentages do not sum to 100 because several vendors appear in a single deal.

## What we win on

- **Mobile capture.** Nobody else lets a field team record offline and sync later. Lantern Logistics chose us for this alone.
- **One tool from inbox to published roadmap.** Teams using Signalbox plus a separate roadmap tool pay more and keep two sources of truth.
- **Price for contributors.** Free unlimited contributors beat every competitor except Loopwise.

## Where we lose

> [!WARNING]
> Clustering is the reason for 14 of our 23 losses. Signalbox and Fathom both produce themes that explain themselves, and ours do not. Until [Spec: Feedback clustering v2](doc:Spec: Feedback clustering v2) ships, we should not lead with clustering in demos.

The other losses were security reviews (five, mostly banks asking for data residency) and incumbency (four, where the buyer already owned a Canopy licence).

## What to watch

Loopwise raised a $40 million round in May and has announced a research module. Canopy is rumoured to be moving to usage-based pricing. If either happens, revisit [Pricing principles](doc:Pricing principles).
