# Interview notes: Northwind Retail

**Date:** 22 September 2026, 50 minutes, on site at their Rotterdam head office
**Interviewed:** Product lead and two store operations managers
**Interviewers:** Priya Nair, Jonas Weber

## Context

Northwind has 140 stores in six countries. Store managers send feedback from the shop floor; head office reviews it weekly. They use Skiff on shared tablets in about 60 stores and Tidepool at head office.

## What we heard

- Store staff type short notes and often lose signal in stockrooms. Offline works, but nobody can tell if a note has synced, so they write it twice.
- Voice notes would be used far more than typing, especially in German and Dutch.
- The product lead spends Monday mornings merging duplicate themes by hand. She estimates two hours a week.
- They want a Slack message when a theme passes 25 items, as their team lives in Slack.

> "My stores don't have time to write. They have time to say a sentence."
