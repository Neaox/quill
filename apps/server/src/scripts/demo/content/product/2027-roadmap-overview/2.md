## How to read this

- **Quarters are commitments only for Q1.** Q2 is a plan and Q3 and Q4 are directions. We will revisit Q2 at the January planning session.
- **Every initiative traces to a bet.** Platform is the exception: it is the work that keeps the other bets possible, and it is capped at 25% of engineering capacity.
- **The Skiff work is sequenced.** Push sync depends on the new architecture work in [RFC-015: Replace polling with push sync](doc:RFC-015: Replace polling with push sync), so it cannot move into Q1 without dropping voice notes.
- **Slack is conditional.** It stays in Q1 only while clustering v2 is on schedule; see [Spec: Slack integration](doc:Spec: Slack integration).

## What is not here

Custom fields, a Jira two-way sync and a Teams integration all had champions in the September session. None made the cut. We will say so when customers ask, and point them at the vision's list of things we will not do.
