# Our values

These came out of three all-hands workshops and a lot of arguing. They are meant to settle real questions, such as whether to ship on Friday or whether to tell a customer the bad news today. When two of them disagree, how we resolve it is in [How we make decisions](doc:How we make decisions).

:::cards{variant=ruled}
### Listen first

Every product we make starts with someone telling us what is wrong. Read the ticket, call the customer, then form an opinion.

### Say the hard thing early

A problem raised on Monday costs an hour. The same problem raised on Friday costs a weekend. Bring bad news to the person who can act on it, kindly and immediately.

### Own the outcome

You are responsible for whether it works for the customer, not only for whether your part shipped. Follow it through to the person using it.

### Keep it simple

The smaller solution that solves the problem wins. We would rather ship a good feature to everyone than a perfect one to nobody.
:::
