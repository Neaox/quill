# Meeting norms

In September we audited the calendars of 60 people and found the average person spent 11 hours a week in meetings, a third of them without an agenda. These norms are how we got that down.

- **Agenda or no meeting.** Every invite says what we need to decide or share, in two lines. If there is no agenda, you can decline.
- **25 and 50.** Default lengths are 25 and 50 minutes, so people can breathe between meetings. Start on time.
- **Fewer, smaller.** Invite only people who will decide or who must act. Everyone else gets the notes. Optional guests really are optional.
- **No-meeting Wednesdays.** Nothing internal on Wednesdays. Customer calls and incidents are exempt.
- **Write it down.** One person takes notes in Quill and shares them within a day, with decisions and owners at the top. If a decision is made, it follows [How we make decisions](doc:How we make decisions).
- **Respect time zones.** Keep meetings inside the team's core hours. If someone has to join at 07:00 or after 18:00 their time, rotate who does.
- **Read before you come.** Pre-reads go out 24 hours ahead. If the document is longer than 10 minutes of reading, say so in the invite.

Standing meetings expire after three months unless the owner renews them. If a meeting has not made a decision in a month, cancel it.
