# Expenses and travel

Spend company money as carefully as you would your own, and not more carefully than that. If a trip or purchase helps you do your job or look after customers, it is almost certainly fine. These limits are here so nobody has to guess.

## Limits

:::table[Standard limits]{width=70%}
| Category | Limit | Notes |
| :------- | ----: | :---- |
| Flights | Economy | Premium economy for flights over 6 hours |
| Hotel, major cities | $220 per night | Taxes included |
| Hotel, elsewhere | $150 per night | Taxes included |
| Meals while travelling | $75 per day | Alcohol is not covered |
| Client meals | $90 per person | Name the guests |
| Software and tools | $50 per month | Above that, ask your manager |
:::

Book flights at least 14 days ahead where you can. Last-minute fares are fine for customer emergencies.

## Approvals

- Up to $500: your manager approves your claim after the fact.
- $501 to $2,500: get your manager's approval before you spend.
- Over $2,500: you also need approval from your VP before you spend.

Customer travel, such as a visit to Harbourview Health or Northwind Retail, needs your manager's approval for the trip, not each item.

## Claiming

Submit claims in the expenses tool with a photo of each receipt. You need a receipt for anything over $25; below that, a note saying what it was is enough. Claims go in within 45 days of the spend. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll, usually within 10 days. Late claims need your VP's sign-off.

For travel that includes time zones that overlap with [on-call duty](doc:On-call compensation), tell your rota owner before you book.

:::details[I lost a receipt. What now?]
Submit the claim anyway with a note saying what you bought, when, and why. For amounts under $25 your manager can approve it as is. Above that, ask the vendor for a copy; most can resend one within a day.
:::

:::details[Can I book my own flights?]
Yes. Use the company card where you have one, or claim it back. You do not need to use a travel agent. Keep fares changeable if the dates might move.
:::

:::details[Can I bring a partner or family member?]
You can, at your own cost. We cover only your own flight, room and meals. If a double room costs more than the single rate, you pay the difference.
:::
