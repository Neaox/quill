# Code of conduct

Everyone at Acme should be able to do good work without being belittled, harassed or made to feel they do not belong. This applies to employees, contractors, interns and anyone representing us, in every place we work together: offices, video calls, chat, email, events and social gatherings. It applies to how we treat customers and each other.

## What we expect

- Treat colleagues and customers with respect, including when you disagree. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Include people. Make room in meetings, credit others' ideas and ask quieter people what they think.
- Respect privacy. Do not share colleagues' personal details, and do not record calls without telling people.
- Take care of customer data as if it were your own. See the information security policy for specifics.

## What we do not tolerate

Harassment, discrimination, bullying and retaliation. That includes unwelcome comments about someone's body, age, race, gender, sexuality, disability, religion or background; sexual attention after someone has said no; threats; and sharing offensive material. A joke is not an excuse. If someone tells you it hurt, stop, and apologise.

Managers hold extra responsibility: if someone tells you about a problem, you must pass it on to people operations within two working days, even if they ask you not to. You can tell them you will do that before they say more.

## How to raise a concern

Choose whichever route feels safest:

1. Talk to the person, if you feel able to.
2. Talk to your manager or any other manager.
3. Email conduct@acme.example, which reaches Dana Okafor and one other member of people operations.
4. Use the anonymous reporting line, which is run by an outside company and is open 24 hours a day.

You do not need proof. Say what happened, when and who was there.

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your report within 2 working days.
- We talk to you first about what you want to happen, and agree who will be told.
- We investigate fairly and as fast as we can, aiming to finish within 30 days. Both sides are heard, and both are updated on progress.
- Outcomes range from a conversation to dismissal. We tell you that we acted, though not always the details.

## Retaliation

Nobody will lose pay, opportunities or standing for raising a concern in good faith, or for helping an investigation. Retaliation is itself a breach of this code and is treated as seriously as the original problem. If you think it is happening, tell Dana or Aisha Khan directly.

This code follows from [Our values](doc:Our values). Where local law gives you stronger protection, the law applies.
