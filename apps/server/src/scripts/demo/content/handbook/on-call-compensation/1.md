# On-call compensation

If you carry a pager for Tidepool or Skiff, you are giving up part of your evenings and weekends, and we pay for that. This policy applies from 1 October 2026 to everyone on an engineering or support rota.

:::table[What we pay]{width=70%}
| Item | Amount |
| :--- | -----: |
| Primary on-call, per week | $600 |
| Secondary on-call, per week | $300 |
| Each public holiday on call | $250 |
| Page answered between 22:00 and 07:00 | $50 |
| Time worked on an incident outside your hours | 1.5 times your hourly rate |
:::

Weeks run from Monday 10:00 to the following Monday 10:00. Payments appear in the payroll following the end of the week.

## Rest after a night page

If a page keeps you awake between 00:00 and 05:00 for more than 30 minutes, you start the next day 4 hours later or take the morning off. Just message your manager. After a whole night on a Sev 1, take the next day off.

## Getting paid

Pages are counted from PagerDuty automatically, so you do not need to claim them. For incident work, log your hours in the incident ticket and your manager approves it within the week. See the [Incident response process](doc:Incident response process) for how incidents are run. Claim costs such as mobile data under [Expenses and travel](doc:Expenses and travel).
