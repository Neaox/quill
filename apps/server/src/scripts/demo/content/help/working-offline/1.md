# Working offline

Skiff works without a connection, so a basement meeting room or a flight does not stop you capturing feedback.

## What works offline

- Writing quick notes and recording voice notes.
- Reading notes you have already saved on your phone.
- Choosing a customer and adding tags.

Voice notes are saved as audio and transcribed once they upload. Until then they show **Waiting to sync** in your **Notes** list.

## What needs a connection

Searching your whole workspace, reading the Tidepool Inbox, and seeing transcripts of recent voice notes all need a connection.

## When you reconnect

Skiff syncs by itself when your phone gets a connection, and the **Waiting to sync** label disappears note by note. To sync straight away, pull down on the **Notes** list.

:::callout{type=note}
Notes are stored on your phone until they upload. If you sign out of Skiff while notes are waiting, it warns you first: signing out would delete them.
:::

Skiff keeps the 200 most recent notes available offline.
