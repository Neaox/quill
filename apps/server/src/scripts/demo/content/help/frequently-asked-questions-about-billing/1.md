# Frequently asked questions about billing

Answers to the questions we hear most. If yours is not here, write to billing@acme.example and a person will reply within one working day.

## Plans and seats

::::accordion{name=billing-faq}
:::details[How are seats counted?]
Admins and Editors each use a seat. Viewers are free. Open **Settings > Billing** to see how many seats you are using; you are billed for the highest number you used that month.
:::
:::details[What does each plan cost?]
Team is $29 per seat per month and Business is $59 per seat per month, billed monthly. Pay yearly and you get two months free. Enterprise is priced by quote. See [Change your plan](doc:Change your plan) for what each includes.
:::
:::details[Is there a free trial?]
The Free plan has no time limit. Team and Business include a 14-day trial of paid features the first time you upgrade; you are not charged if you cancel before it ends.
:::
::::

## Paying

::::accordion{name=billing-faq-paying}
:::details[Which payment methods do you accept?]
Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Business and Enterprise workspaces can also pay by invoice with 30-day terms, on request.
:::
:::details[Where do I find my invoices?]
Open **Settings > Billing > Invoices**. Each one has a **Download PDF** button. Invoices are also emailed to the billing contact when they are issued.
:::
:::details[How do I change the email or company name on invoices?]
In **Settings > Billing**, select **Billing details**, edit the fields and select **Save**. Past invoices keep the details they were issued with; write to us if one needs reissuing.
:::
:::details[My payment failed. What happens now?]
We retry after 3 days and again after 7 days, and email the billing contact each time. Your workspace keeps working during that time. If the payment still fails, it moves to the Free plan; nothing is deleted, but anything over the Free limits becomes read-only.
:::
::::

## Cancelling and refunds

::::accordion{name=billing-faq-cancel}
:::details[How do I cancel?]
Open **Settings > Billing**, select **Change plan** and choose **Free**. You keep paid features until the end of the period you paid for.
:::
:::details[Can I get a refund?]
Monthly charges are not refunded part way through a month. If you were charged by mistake, or upgraded by accident, write to us within 14 days and we will put it right.
:::
::::
