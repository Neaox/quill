# Create your first workspace

A workspace is where your team's feedback, themes and roadmap live. Most companies need only one, and setting it up takes about two minutes.

1. Go to tidepool.app and select **Start for free**.
2. Enter your work email and choose a password, or select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
3. Open the email we send you and select **Confirm email address**. The link works for 24 hours; if it has expired, sign in and select **Resend confirmation**.
4. On the **Create a workspace** screen, type a **Workspace name**. Your company or product name works well.
5. Check the **Workspace address**. We fill it in from the name, for example `northwind.tidepool.app`. It can use 3 to 30 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.
6. Select **Create workspace**.

You land on an empty **Inbox**. That is normal: nothing is lost, there is just nothing in it yet.

## What to do next

- Add your first piece of feedback by selecting **Add feedback** at the top right.
- Bring in a backlog with [Import feedback from a CSV file](doc:Import feedback from a CSV file).
- Ask colleagues to join; [Invite your team](doc:Invite your team) shows how.

:::callout{type=note}
New workspaces start on the Free plan: up to 3 people and 1,000 pieces of feedback. You can change plan at any time.
:::
