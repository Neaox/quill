# Record a voice note

A voice note is the fastest way to capture what a customer says while you are still in the room. Skiff records it, then turns it into a written transcript you can search and group like any other feedback.

## Record

1. Open Skiff and select the microphone button at the bottom of the **Capture** tab.
2. Allow microphone access if Skiff asks.
3. Speak naturally. A timer shows how long you have been recording; a note can be up to 10 minutes.
4. Select **Stop** when you are done.
5. Optionally pick a **Customer** and add a tag, then select **Save**.

If you are using a tablet with a keyboard, press :kbd[Space] to start and stop, and :kbd[Esc] to discard the recording.

## What happens next

The note appears at once in your **Notes** list with the label **Transcribing**. A transcript usually follows within a minute or two once you have a connection. Open the note to read it, fix a misheard word by tapping the text, or play the audio back.

The finished note is added to your workspace **Inbox** with the source **Skiff**.

:::callout{type=tip}
Say the customer's name and company at the start of the note. It makes the transcript easier to find later.
:::

No signal? Notes are saved on your phone and uploaded later; see [Working offline](doc:Working offline).
