# Why isn't my feedback appearing?

You sent or imported feedback and it is not in your **Inbox**. Nearly every case has one of the causes below, so work through the steps in order.

## Check the basics

1. Open **Feedback > Inbox** and clear every filter. A saved view or a source filter is the most common reason something looks missing.
2. Search for a word you know is in the feedback.
3. Select **Archived** in the left-hand list. Feedback that was archived, or marked as spam, does not show in the main Inbox.
4. Check your plan. A Free workspace holds up to 1,000 pieces of feedback; beyond that new feedback is refused until you make room or upgrade.
5. Wait a few minutes. Widget messages and Skiff notes can take up to 5 minutes; Zendesk checks every 15 minutes.

## If it still is not there

Find your source below.

:::details[Feedback sent from the widget]
Open **Settings > Widget > Allowed domains** and make sure the site where you tested is in the list, including `www.` if you use it. The widget will not send from a domain that is not allowed, and visitors see **Couldn't send your message**.

If your site has a Content Security Policy, check that `https://widget.tidepool.app` is allowed under `connect-src`. Your browser's developer console shows a blocked request in red.
:::

:::details[A CSV import]
Open **Feedback > Import > Import history** and look at the status of your import. **Failed** means a row had a problem, and the message tells you which one, for example `Row 14: created_at must be a date`. **Completed** with a high **Skipped** count means the rows were duplicates of feedback you already have. See [Import feedback from a CSV file](doc:Import feedback from a CSV file) for the rules.
:::

:::details[Zendesk tickets]
Check three things. The ticket has the tag you chose under **Settings > Integrations > Zendesk**. The ticket was created after you connected, or within the 90 days Tidepool imports on the first sync. The connection still says **Connected**; if it says **Needs attention**, select it and sign in to Zendesk again.
:::

:::details[Skiff notes]
Open Skiff and look for **Waiting to sync** on the note. If it is there, the note is still on your phone: check you have a connection and pull down on the **Notes** list. See [Working offline](doc:Working offline).
:::

## Still stuck?

Write to support@acme.example with the date and time you sent the feedback, where it came from and, if you have it, a screenshot of any message you saw. We can search our logs for it.
