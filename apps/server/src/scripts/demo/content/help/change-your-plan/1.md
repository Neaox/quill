# Change your plan

You can move between Free, Team and Business whenever you like. Only Admins can change plans.

:::table[Tidepool plans]{width=wide}
| Plan | Price | Best for |
| :--- | ----: | :------- |
| Free | $0 | Trying Tidepool: 3 people, 1,000 pieces of feedback |
| Team | $29 per seat per month | Unlimited feedback and a public roadmap |
| Business | $59 per seat per month | Custom domains, single sign-on and an audit log |
| Enterprise | Quoted | Larger rollouts, security reviews and custom contracts |
:::

## Upgrade or downgrade

1. Open **Settings > Billing**.
2. Select **Change plan**.
3. Choose a plan and the number of seats, then select **Review changes**.
4. Check the summary and select **Confirm**.

An upgrade starts straight away and you pay only for the days left in the month. A downgrade takes effect at the end of your billing period, so you keep what you have paid for until then.

Enterprise is arranged with our team: use **Contact sales** on the same page.

Before you downgrade to Free, make sure you have no more than 3 people and fewer than 1,000 pieces of feedback; otherwise Tidepool asks you to remove some first. Questions about invoices and refunds are answered in [Frequently asked questions about billing](doc:Frequently asked questions about billing).
