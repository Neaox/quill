# Group feedback into themes

A theme is a short name for something several customers keep asking for, such as "Export to PDF" or "Slower than it should be". Grouping feedback into themes turns a long inbox into a ranked list you can act on.

## Add feedback to a theme

1. Open **Feedback > Inbox**.
2. Tick the box beside each piece of feedback that belongs together. Hold :kbd[Shift] and select a second box to tick everything in between.
3. Select **Add to theme** in the bar that appears at the bottom.
4. Choose an existing theme, or type a name and select **Create theme**.

The theme page shows every piece of feedback, how many different customers raised it, and which plans they are on. A theme counts each customer once, however many times they write in.

## Let Tidepool suggest themes

When you have at least 50 pieces of feedback, a banner above the **Inbox** says **Suggested themes**. Select it to see groups of similar feedback that Tidepool found, each with a proposed name. Choose **Accept** to create the theme, **Edit** to change the name first, or **Dismiss** to hide the suggestion. Suggestions refresh overnight and never move feedback without you saying so.

## Keep your themes tidy

Open **Feedback > Themes** to see them all, ranked by the number of customers. From there you can:

- **Rename** a theme. The new name is used everywhere, including on any public roadmap.
- **Merge** two themes that mean the same thing. Select both, then choose **Merge themes** and pick which name to keep.
- **Split** a theme that has become too broad by selecting some of its feedback and choosing **Move to another theme**.

:::callout{type=tip}
Name themes after the customer's problem, not your solution. "Hard to find old feedback" leaves room for ideas; "Add search" does not.
:::

## Common questions

::::accordion{name=themes-faq}
:::details[Can one piece of feedback be in two themes?]
Yes. A long interview note often mentions several things. Add it to each theme it covers and it counts once in each.
:::
:::details[What happens to feedback if I delete a theme?]
The feedback goes back to your **Inbox**, marked as unsorted. Nothing is deleted. Any roadmap item linked to the theme loses the link but keeps its title.
:::
:::details[How many themes should I have?]
Most teams are happy with 20 to 40 active themes. If you have hundreds, merge the small ones; a theme with a single customer is usually better left in the **Inbox** until a second one appears.
:::
::::

When a theme is ready to become a commitment, you can show it to customers with [Publish a public roadmap](doc:Publish a public roadmap).
