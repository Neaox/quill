# Invite your team

Tidepool works best when support, product and design all see the same feedback. Anyone with the Admin role can invite people.

## Send invitations

1. Open **Settings > People**.
2. Select **Invite people**.
3. Type or paste up to 50 email addresses, separated by commas or new lines.
4. Choose a role for the group: **Admin**, **Editor** or **Viewer**.
5. Select **Send invitations**.

Each person gets an email with a link that works for 7 days. Until they accept, they appear in the list as **Pending**. Select **Resend** next to a name if the email went astray, or **Revoke** to cancel it.

## What each role can do

- **Admin**: everything, including billing, integrations and removing people.
- **Editor**: add, tag and group feedback, and edit the roadmap.
- **Viewer**: read everything and comment, but not change it.

:::callout{type=tip}
Not sure which role to pick? Start people as **Viewer** and promote them later from the same page. Viewers are free on every plan and do not count towards your seats.
:::

If a colleague says they never received the email, ask them to check their spam folder for a message from `invites@tidepool.app`. On the Free plan you can have 3 people in total, including pending invitations.

Once everyone is in, try [Group feedback into themes](doc:Group feedback into themes) together.
