# Publish a public roadmap

A public roadmap shows customers what you are working on and lets them vote for what matters to them. You decide which items appear; nothing is public until you publish it.

## Prepare your roadmap

Every roadmap item sits in one of three columns: **Planned**, **In progress** and **Shipped**. Before you publish, open **Roadmap** and check that each item has a clear title, a one or two sentence description, and a column. Items marked **Private** never appear publicly, so use that for anything internal.

## Publish it

1. Open **Roadmap > Settings**.
2. Under **Visibility**, switch **Public roadmap** on.
3. Choose a **Page title** and a short **Introduction** for visitors.
4. Select which columns to show. Most teams hide **Planned** until they are confident about dates.
5. Select **Preview** to see the page as a visitor does.
6. Select **Publish**.

Your roadmap is now live at `your-workspace.tidepool.app/roadmap`. Copy the address from the top of the **Settings** page and share it.

:::callout[Votes and comments]{type=note}
Visitors vote by entering their email address and confirming it from a link. Votes appear on the item and on the theme it came from, so a vote counts as feedback. Switch **Allow comments** off in **Roadmap > Settings** if you only want votes.
:::

## Keep it up to date

Dragging an item to another column updates the public page within a minute. When you move an item to **Shipped**, everyone who voted for it gets an email, so make sure the description is something you are happy to send.

To take the page down, switch **Public roadmap** off. Votes are kept, and the page returns the same way when you switch it back on.

If you have not grouped your feedback yet, start with [Group feedback into themes](doc:Group feedback into themes): linking a theme to an item is what brings its customers' votes across.
