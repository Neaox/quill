# Install Skiff and sign in

Skiff is Tidepool's companion app for capturing feedback on the move. It is free with every Tidepool plan.

## What you need

- An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an Android phone with Android 12 or later.
- An account in a Tidepool workspace. Ask an admin to [invite you](doc:Invite your team) if you do not have one.

## Install

On iPhone, open the App Store and search for **Skiff by Acme**. On Android, open Google Play and search for the same name. Select **Get** or **Install**.

## Sign in

1. Open Skiff and select **Sign in**.
2. Enter your work email and select **Continue**.
3. Skiff sends a six-digit code to that address. Type it in. The code works for 10 minutes.
4. If your company uses single sign-on, you are sent to your company's sign-in page instead. Sign in there and you return to Skiff automatically.

If you belong to more than one workspace, choose one on the next screen. You can switch later from **Settings > Workspace**.

The first time you open Skiff it asks for permission to use the microphone and to send notifications. You can say no to notifications, but you need the microphone for voice notes. If you tapped **Don't allow** by mistake, change it in your phone's settings under **Skiff**.
