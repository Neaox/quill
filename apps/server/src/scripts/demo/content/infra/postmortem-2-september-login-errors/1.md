# Postmortem: 2 September login errors

:small[Severity SEV2. Written by Aisha Khan and Marcus Lee, 4 September 2026. Blameless format.]

## Summary

On Wednesday 2 September 2026, from 14:03 to 14:41 UTC, customers who sign in through single sign-on could not log in. About 4,300 SSO sign-in attempts failed across 190 workspaces, including Pinecrest Bank and Harbourview Health. Password and magic-link logins, and people who were already signed in, were not affected. The cause was a scheduled secret rotation in which the old SSO broker client secret was revoked before the pods had loaded the new one.

## What happened

The rotation of `tidepool/prod/sso-broker-client` was due that week. The engineer doing it followed the runbook as it stood: create a new secret, promote it, and remove the old one in the identity provider. Two details in our setup turned that into an outage.

- The External Secrets Operator refreshed every hour, so a new value could take up to 60 minutes to reach the Kubernetes Secret.
- The `api` deployment was not annotated for Stakater Reloader. Even after the Secret changed, the pods would have kept running with the old value in memory until their next restart.

The runbook did not warn about either, and it had no overlap period. The old secret was revoked at 14:03, and the identity provider began answering our token requests with `invalid_client`.

## Root cause

We revoked a credential on the other side of an integration without any period in which both values worked, and we assumed that promoting the new value in Secrets Manager meant the running service had it. Neither assumption was checked by the runbook or by a test.

## What went well

- The burn-rate alert fired six minutes after the first failure and paged a person.
- Existing sessions are stored in PostgreSQL, so no one who was signed in was logged out.
- Aisha Khan phoned Pinecrest Bank's security contact within the hour, before they wrote in.

## What did not

- It took 13 minutes from the page to the `invalid_client` log line, and 22 to connect it to our own change. We suspected the identity provider first and checked our own change last, even though a rotation had been announced that morning.
- Nobody on the rotation ticket was told to watch the login rate afterwards.

## Action items

- [x] Add an overlap period for every secret and forbid revoking in the same step. Done 5 September, in [Secrets rotation](doc:Secrets rotation).
- [x] Annotate all deployments that mount secrets with `reloader.stakater.com/auto: "true"`. Done 4 September.
- [x] Lower the External Secrets refresh interval from 60 to 10 minutes. Done 4 September.
- [x] Add "Is a rotation or deploy in progress?" as the first question in the on-call guide. Done 9 September.
